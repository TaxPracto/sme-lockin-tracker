Lead managers

3 graded · 32 tracked in total · lead manager known for 77 of 77 tracked SME IPOs · unlock history covers 24 Jan 2025 to 03 Jul 2026 · updated 12 Aug 2026, 09:06 IST
What this page tells you. The lead manager (merchant banker) designs an SME IPO — picks the company, sets the price, builds the anchor book, underwrites the issue and appoints the market maker. Funds come and go deal by deal; the lead manager is the one actor present in every one of its issues. This page grades each of them purely on what happened to their issues' prices after lock-ins opened — no opinions, only measured outcomes.
Reading a row: "8 issues · 19 unlocks watched · −2.1% · 58% · 43% below issue" means: 8 of its IPOs are in our tracked universe; 19 of their unlocks have passed; the typical stock moved −2.1% in the week after; 58 out of 100 fell; and 43% of its priced issues trade below issue price today.

Why can "unlocks watched" exceed "issues"? Every IPO creates several unlocks — two anchor tranches, pre-IPO holders at 6 months, promoter slices later. All of them count here.
The verdict: HOLDS UP = issues usually stay firm after their unlock days. FADES = issues usually slip after unlock days — be careful holding its issues through unlock dates. MIXED = in between. A lead manager needs at least 4 issues with measured unlocks to get a verdict — everyone else waits in the "not yet graded" list below the table.
