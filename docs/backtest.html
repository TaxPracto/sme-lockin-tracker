Unlock backtest

128 past unlock events measured, covering 24 Jan 2025 to 03 Jul 2026 · official BSE/NSE closing prices · refreshed daily, last: 03 Aug 2026, 10:32 IST · ← back to the radar
How to read this page. For every lock-in that opened in the past, we noted the share price just before the unlock day and again 5 trading days later. "Typical move" is the middle result — half the stocks did better, half did worse (one crazy stock can't distort it). "Fell how often" is simply: out of 100 such unlocks, how many stocks were lower a week later.
Example: a row reading "90D anchor · 53 · −1.9% · 58%" means: we watched 53 ninety-day unlocks; the typical stock was down 1.9% a week later; 58 out of 100 fell. So 90-day unlocks have been bad news on average.
